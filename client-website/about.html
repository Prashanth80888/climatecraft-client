Our origin

A mechanism, then a chair

Climate Craft began in a small Antwerp workshop repairing the motion mechanisms other makers couldn't. We learned that the mechanism — not the fabric — is what fails first, and what a piece lives or dies by.

So we build the other way around: the motor and the glide come first, tested to 30,000 cycles, and the frame and upholstery are built to honour them.

Our vision

Motion, made to last a generation.

We picture a world where powered furniture is no longer disposable — where a mechanism is engineered, serviced and trusted like the movement of a fine watch. Seating that adapts to the body, silently, for decades.

Our mission

Engineer the quiet, wrap it beautifully.

To build the most reliable motion mechanisms in the industry and dress them in an upholstery worthy of the engineering underneath — supplying the manufacturers, galleries and houses who refuse to compromise on either.

How we work

From mechanism to made.

01 / Consult

Specify

We start with your brief — operation, dimensions, fabric direction and volume. Trade partners receive a dedicated project lead from day one.

02 / Frame

Build & test

The mechanism is assembled and the hardwood frame built around it. Every motor and glide is cycle-tested before leaving the frame shop.

03 / Dress

Upholster

High-resilience foam and feather-down are wrapped by hand, before a single maker cuts, sews and fits your chosen fabric.

04 / Ship

Deliver

Pieces are crated securely and shipped directly to site or receiving warehouse. White-glove installation available in select regions.
