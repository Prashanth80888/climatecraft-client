Home Theatre Case Study

The Ultimate Home Theatre Recliner

Creating an acoustically transparent, temperature-controlled cinema seating row for 135-inch 4K projection marathons in Bengaluru.

Executive Overview

In a private residence in Dollars Colony, Bengaluru, AV enthusiast Kabir built a dedicated 7.1.4 Dolby Atmos cinema with a 135-inch 4K Laser Projector screen. To complement the reference-grade AV setup, Kabir required bespoke cinema recliners engineered for 3- to 5-hour movie sessions.

The Cinema Comfort Challenge

Traditional theatre recliners cause thermal discomfort during long double-feature screenings, while noisy electric motors ruin subtle audio passages. Requirements included:

  • Sub-25dB whisper-quiet motor operation during recline adjustments.
  • Active cooling to prevent heat buildup during multi-hour movie marathons.
  • Voice and dark-mode touchscreen controls so lighting levels are never disturbed.

The Climate Craft Cinema Installation

Climate Craft built a curved row of 4 Cinema Series Smart Electric Recliners finished in matte black acoustic velvet and leather accents.

Cinema Spec Sheet:

Acoustic Motor Dampening: Custom insulated motor housings operating at an ultra-quiet 22dB.
Dark-Mode Touchscreen: OLED armrest interface with auto-dimming backlighting.
Hydro Thermal Control: Gentle 18°C cooling or 32°C warming for long viewing sessions.
Integrated Cupholder Coolers: Active Peltier cup chillers built into solid aluminum armrests.

Homeowner Review

"The silent motors and temperature regulation completely change the movie watching experience. You never feel fidgety or warm halfway through a 3-hour film," says Kabir.
