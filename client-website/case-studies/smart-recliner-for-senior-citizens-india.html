Senior Mobility Case Study

Smart Recliner for Senior Citizens in India

Combining gentle motorized power-lift assistance with therapeutic warmth for joint stiffness relief in Sadashivanagar.

Executive Overview

74-year-old retired professor Dr. Murthy residing in Sadashivanagar, Bengaluru, suffered from joint stiffness and osteoarthritis. Sitting down into deep conventional armchairs and standing back up presented physical strain and fall hazards.

Senior Comfort & Safety Requirements

Mobility seating for senior family members must prioritize safety, leg support, and ease of control above all else:

  • A smooth, tilt-and-lift mechanism that gently assists the user into a full standing position.
  • Gentle, therapeutic lumbar heat (34°C–38°C) to ease joint stiffness on cool mornings.
  • Large, tactile remote controls with high-contrast backlit buttons for easy operation.

The Climate Craft Care Series Installation

Climate Craft delivered the Senior Care Series Power-Lift Recliner in supple, non-slip cognac leather with high-density ergonomic foam density.

Care Series Spec Sheet

  • Power Stand-Assist Lift Smooth, stable tilt motor rated up to 160kg
  • Therapeutic Heat Therapy Targeted 38°C spinal joint warmth

  • Emergency Battery Backup Uninterrupted operation during power outages
