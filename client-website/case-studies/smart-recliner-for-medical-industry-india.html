Healthcare & Commercial Case Study

Smart Recliner for the Medical Industry in India

Elevating VIP visitor suites and executive physician lounges in a premier Bengaluru multi-specialty clinic with medical-grade leather and liquid climate controls.

Executive Overview

A leading private multi-specialty healthcare center in Cunningham Road, Bengaluru, sought to modernize its executive doctor rest lounges and VIP patient waiting suites. The medical director aimed to offer premium corporate visitors and senior consultants an exceptional level of quiet, climate-controlled relaxation.

Commercial Healthcare Requirements

Commercial healthcare facilities require furniture built to stringent hygiene and acoustic standards while exuding warmth and luxury:

  • Antimicrobial, wipe-clean performance upholstery suitable for clinical environments.
  • Whisper-quiet motor actuation (<25dB) ensuring a peaceful waiting lounge environment.
  • Intuitive touch controls with clear visual temperature indicators for effortless operation.

The Climate Craft Healthcare Lounge Solution

Climate Craft supplied 6 Commercial Lounge Electric Recliners outfitted with antimicrobial, liquid-resistant synthetic leather in slate grey.

Specification Highlights:

Antimicrobial Healthcare Coating: Impermeable to liquids and easily disinfected with medical wipes.
Silent Motor Operation: Ultra-quiet micro-stepper motors maintaining tranquility.
Liquid Cooling & Warming: Temperature settings from 15°C to 35°C for personalized visitor comfort.
Modular Maintenance Design: Toolless quick-swap thermal cassettes for easy servicing.

Medical Facility Director Feedback

"The Climate Craft lounge chairs have transformed our VIP waiting suite into a world-class environment. Consultants and international patients frequently comment on the incredible comfort of the active cooling seats," notes Dr. Suresh Rao, Managing Director.
