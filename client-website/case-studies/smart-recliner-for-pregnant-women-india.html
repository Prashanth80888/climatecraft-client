Maternity Wellness Case Study

Smart Recliner for Pregnant Women in India

Providing gentle lumbar support, effortless motorized positioning, and cooling comfort for expecting and nursing mothers in Koramangala.

Executive Overview

During her second trimester, Meera from Koramangala experienced persistent lower back strain and night hot flashes common in humid Indian climates. Standard living room seating provided insufficient lower spine support and required physical effort to exit.

Maternity Health & Ergonomic Considerations

Pregnancy requires gentle, highly adaptable seating designed around biomechanical body changes:

  • Smooth, effortless motorized recline requiring zero abdominal core pressure to adjust.
  • Sustained 21°C surface cooling to soothe maternal overheating without cold drafts.
  • Adjustable lumbar fill to alleviate lower back lordosis strain.
  • Gentle power-assist lift to help standing up safely without physical exertion.

The Climate Craft Maternity Solution

Climate Craft customized a Maternity Series Electric Glider & Recliner wrapped in ultra-soft, hypoallergenic, breathable natural leather in cream white.

Maternity Feature Spec

  • Effortless Power Assist Lift Gentle standing assistance without abdominal strain
  • Micro-Adjustable Lumbar Support Motorized air-cell lumbar bladder
  • Sustained Surface Cooling Soothing 21°C temperature regulation
  • Hypoallergenic Materials Zero VOC finishes & natural leather

Mother's Experience

"The power-lift feature and subtle cooling made my late trimester and early nursing weeks infinitely easier. It is the most comfortable seat in our home," shares Meera.
