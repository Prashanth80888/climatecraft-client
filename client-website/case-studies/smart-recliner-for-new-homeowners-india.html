Residential & Lifestyle Case Study

Smart Recliner for New Homeowners in India

A first home living room anchor combining bespoke Italian leather upholstery, dual-zone temperature control, and quiet electric recline.

Executive Overview

When Ananya and Rohan purchased their first 3BHK high-rise apartment in Whitefield, Bengaluru, they sought furniture that reflected their progressive taste while delivering unmatched personal comfort. They wanted a central living room sofa that offered both sleek modern aesthetics and state-of-the-art relaxation.

The Design & Comfort Objectives

Most standard recliners in India are bulky, outdated, and offer only manual or basic motor controls without temperature regulation. Ananya and Rohan needed:

  • Independent dual-zone temperature control so one person could cool down while the other enjoyed warmth.
  • A refined architectural profile that complemented their contemporary interior palette.
  • Zero-wall electric recline mechanisms to maximize living room space efficiency.

The Climate Craft 2-Seater Solution

The couple commissioned a customized Climate Craft 2-Seater Smart Recliner Sofa in Warm Bone aniline leather with dark teal trim.

Bespoke Features Installed:

Dual Independent Thermal Zones: Each seat controls its own hydro-cooling (down to 15°C) and warming (up to 35°C).
Concealed Touch Interfaces: Armrest touchscreen controls integrated seamlessly into the leather structure.
Silent Motor Glide: Wall-hugger mechanism requiring only 8cm of wall clearance.
Integrated Wireless Charging: Flush wireless smartphone pads in each armrest.

Homeowner Feedback

"Guests are routinely amazed when they sit down and experience active cooling through natural leather. It turned our living room into the ultimate weekend gathering space," notes Rohan. The dual thermal controls resolved their temperature preference differences effortlessly.
