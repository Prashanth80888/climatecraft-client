Luxury Interiors Case Study

Luxury Home Furniture India – Elevating Living

Outfitting a 6,000 sq. ft. architectural villa in Bengaluru with custom climate-controlled smart seating for three generations.

Executive Overview

Designing furniture for a multi-generational 6,000 sq. ft. villa in Jayanagar, Bengaluru required bridging aesthetic architectural elegance with diverse comfort demands. The residence accommodates a business executive couple, their two teenagers, and elderly parents.

The Architectural & Multi-User Vision

The interior architect sought furniture pieces that could serve as sculptural room accents while offering tailored thermal environments for each family member:

  • A central 3-seater smart recliner sofa for family movie nights.
  • A formal 2-seater suite for receiving guests in the living hall.
  • Single electric climate recliners for reading nooks and senior bedroom lounges.

The Climate Craft Complete Villa Installation

Climate Craft delivered a cohesive suite of handcrafted furniture wrapped in Italian full-grain hides in muted sage and bone tones, seamlessly integrating quiet motors and liquid thermal loops.

Villa Furniture Collection Installed:

Smart 3-Seater Recliner Sofa: Triple-zone hydro cooling & warming with voice control.
Smart 2-Seater Living Room Sofa: Concealed capacitive touch controls and wireless phone charging.
Single Executive Recliners: Tailored ergonomics for senior family members.
Unified Smart Home Integration: Integrated into Crestron and Control4 automation systems.

Architect & Client Response

"Climate Craft gave us the rare combination of haute couture furniture design and genuine high-technology. Every family member gets their preferred temperature and recline angle at the push of a button," states lead architect Rajesh Menon.
