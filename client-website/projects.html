Challenges & solutions

The challenge

A moving target

The client needed a consistent motorized recliner across a flagship still being built — with no fixed delivery date and a fit-out schedule that kept shifting.

  • Undefined final quantities at kickoff
  • Single bespoke fabric across all units
  • Phased, just-in-time delivery
Our solution

Made-to-order, staged

We locked the mechanism and fabric early, then built to a rolling forecast — releasing tranches against the site calendar with weekly milestone reporting.

  • Cycle-tested mechanism signed off in week one
  • Fabric archived and colour-locked for reorders
  • Dedicated project lead end-to-end
